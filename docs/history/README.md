## Current interpretation

Homework uses Pazo/Palatino mathematics with Pagella prose.
Notes use Pagella mathematics with separate AMS Euler chapter numerals.
All six courses use the shared classes.
The former testbed and temporary migration worktrees are no longer present.

Earlier descriptions of Unicode homework, Euler notes, or retained worktrees describe historical checkpoints.
Their measurements and source revisions remain valid records of those checkpoints.
The [known issues](../KNOWN-ISSUES.md) document connects maintenance findings to their evidence.

The [evidence manifests](../README.md#evidence-manifests) remain at their original paths.
Historical paths inside manifests and command examples retain their original meaning.
